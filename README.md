## Doctrine

1. No strong recommendation at low confidence.
2. No fake changes. Only high-value, safe improvements.
3. Every decision is a trace: input, evidence, anomalies, confidence, status.
4. Layers stay separate. Measurement does not decide. Decision does not recommend.
5. Priority = impact × feasibility × strategic × reusability.
